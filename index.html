It started as an outlet.

"At first, it was just a place to switch my head off and work hard."

Chris left university in 2016 feeling lost, unfit and low on confidence. Boxing changed that. It gave him structure, discipline and a belief in himself he hadn't felt before.

He became a personal trainer to help other people feel the same. Nine years later, that's grown into BootyBox. Not from a business plan, but from caring about every single person who walks in.

He became a Dad in September 2025, which he'll tell you is the best thing that's ever happened to him, and he has another on the way. It's made him even more sure of what BootyBox is for: helping women feel strong, confident and capable in lives that are already busy.
